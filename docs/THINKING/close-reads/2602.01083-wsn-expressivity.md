# Close-read: On the Expressive Power of Permutation-Equivariant Weight-Space Networks (arXiv:2602.01083, Feb 2026)

**Read:** full text (theorem-targeted), 2026-07-17. **Role:** PO-6 novelty check.

## What they prove

Scope: **ReLU MLPs, permutation symmetries only** (zero occurrences of sine/periodic; SIREN only in
passing). Results: (Thm 5.2 + Prop 5.3) all prominent perm-equivariant WSN families (DWS, NFN,
NG-GNN; NFT under a generic-position assumption) are expressively equivalent. (Thm 6.1) invariant
WSNs approximate any continuous **function-space** functional on compact sets. (Prop 6.2)
counterexample: a perm-invariant *weight-space* map not approximable in general — two 1-WL-
indistinguishable binary weight configurations with different rank structure. (Thm 6.3) off a
degenerate exclusion set E (pairwise-distinct bias entries), any perm-invariant weight-space
functional is approximable — proof constructs a **continuous canonicalization map on K ⊂ V∖E**
(sorting is continuous off ties). (Thm 7.2/7.4, Prop 7.1/7.3) equivariant/operator versions,
incl. natural function-space operators that are *not* approximable for fixed ReLU architectures.

## Relation to PO-6 (verdict: PO-6 survives, framing sharpened)

They prove **approximation** universality for the permutation group on ReLU nets. PO-6 is a
different statement: assuming identifiability (PO-2), any **exact complete invariant** of the full
sine symmetry group factors through the realization map — an information-content statement with the
informational-vs-computational corollary for weight-only perception. Not contained in their
results; but the *spirit* (weight-space maps that respect symmetries ≈ function-space maps) is
theirs for the ReLU/perm case and must be cited as such. Paper A phrasing: "for permutation
symmetries of ReLU networks, [2602.01083] establish the approximation-theoretic counterpart; we
give the exact, identifiability-based statement for the full D∞ ≀ Sₙ group of sine networks."
