## What they do

off a proper real algebraic variety ε, functional equality implies group-relatedness — exactly the
formalization PO-2 needs (adopt this phrasing, cite them). (3.1) quasi-equivariance: F(gθ) =
g′(g,θ)·F(θ) — output group element may depend on input; preserves functional identity while
relaxing strict equivariance. Construction: learned group actions near identity via
sin(γ(θ))·ε + I ∈ GL(n) — **their "sine" is an ingredient of the learned-action parameterization,
not the activation of the processed networks.** Symmetry scope inherited from Monomial-NFN
(perm × sign/scaling) + GL actions for attention blocks. Phase symmetries of periodic activations:
absent (0 occurrences of "phase"; no bias-shift maps anywhere).
