# Close-read: Improved Generalization of WS Networks via Augmentations (Shamsian et al., ICML 2024, arXiv:2402.04081)

**Read:** full text, 2026-07-17. **Role:** Gate-1 revision source; anchor A2 origin; W6 design input.

## What they do

Taxonomy: (i) input-space augs implemented through weights (Translate: b₁←b₁+W₁t; Rotation: W₁←RW₁,
φ~U(−30°,30°); Scale: c~U(0.8,1)) — these **change the represented object** (data augmentation
proper); (ii) data-agnostic (noise, masking, quantile masking); (iii) **activation-symmetry augs**
(function-preserving, nuisance augmentation): "SIREN negation" = our σ (flip Wᵢ, bᵢ and Wᵢ₊₁), and
**"SIREN bias" = our ρ/τ**: b̃ᵢ = bᵢ + kπ, W̃ᵢ₊₁ = (−1)ᵏWᵢ₊₁, k ∈ ℤ. Plus weight-space MixUp
(direct / +perm / +alignment), which is their headline method (≈ "up to 10× more data").

## The result that matters most to us (their Table 3, appendix)

**SIREN bias augmentation actively damages weight-space learners.** DWS: 4.69 vs ≈18 (no-aug,
ModelNet40-INR low-view), 58.2 vs ≈68 (FMNIST), 24.3 vs ≈39 (CIFAR-family). GNN degrades less but
still mostly below no-aug. SIREN negation (σ) is mildly helpful on some cells. No bounded-k
variant, no bias-statistics matching, no analysis of *why* it fails is given.

## Gate-1 consequence (novelty statement revised, not killed)

The τ/ρ symmetry *formulas* are published here (Feb 2024) — Gate-1's strict reading ("does anything
exploit sine translation symmetries?") = "yes, as augmentation, and it failed." The program's
actual bets remain untouched and are now *sharpened by published evidence*:
- **Exactness vs averaging:** canonicalization removes the nuisance; augmentation asks the decoder
  to average over an unbounded orbit direction — their Table 3 is direct evidence the latter fails
  for the phase component, consistent with PO-12's effective-orbit-size logic.
- Their negative result is our W6 design constraint: protocol already mandates τ range matched to
  empirical bias statistics; the memo for S1 must cite their unbounded-k failure as the motivation
  and treat "does bounded-τ augmentation rescue it?" as a genuine question (either answer is
  informative for RQ4's discriminator).
