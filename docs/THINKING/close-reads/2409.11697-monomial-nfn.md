# Close-read: Monomial-NFN (Tran et al., NeurIPS 2024, arXiv:2409.11697)

**Read:** full text, 2026-07-17. **Role:** closest theory+architecture neighbor; Gate-1 near-miss.

## What they prove and build

- Prop 3.4: the maximal **matrix subgroup of GL(n)** preserved by an activation (in the intertwining
  sense) is G⁺ₙ (positive monomial) for ReLU and G±¹ₙ (signed permutation) for sin and tanh. They
  note variants existed before ([24, 68] in their numbering) and they refine the statement.
- Prop 4.4: the induced per-layer group product leaves FCNN/CNN functions invariant; weight-space
  action is **linear** throughout (monomial matrices acting on each layer's (W, b)).
- Remark 4.5 (the crux for us): maximality of this group **as the full functional symmetry group**
  is proved only for (a) ReLU under a width-monotonicity condition (citing [12, 25]) and (b) tanh
  (citing [14, 22]). For **sin they state it as an open question**: "It is natural to ask whether
  the group G is still maximal in the other case. This question still remains open and we leave it
  for future exploration."
- Architecture: equivariant/invariant linear layers for the monomial group; fewer parameters than
  perm-only NFNs; INR classification among the experiments.

## Delta (precise, fair)

1. Their open question has a **negative answer as posed in the linear-action world and a complete
   answer in the affine world**: bias phase maps τ: b↦b+2π and ρ: (b,u)↦(b+π,−u) preserve f, are
   affine (not monomial/linear), and act generically — so G±¹ₙ⋊perms is *not* the full symmetry
   group of sine networks. PO-1 states the correct group (D∞ ≀ Sₙ per layer); PO-2 (L=1) will show
   it is everything on the generic stratum. Our Paper A should open with exactly this resolution.
2. Architecture delta: Monomial-NFN is equivariant to σ+perm only. Our Ch3.7 layer adds the
   phase component (cos 2b / sin 2b circular channels + the ρ-consistent sign coupling), giving the
   perm/+sign/+phase ablation (F19) a clean published baseline as its middle rung.
3. Caution for our claims: their maximality language is about *matrix* groups; when writing Paper A
   we must not imply they claimed functional maximality for sine — they explicitly did not.
