**G5 update to row 15 (weakest link).** PO-2's deep case remains the *theoretical* weakest link and
is unchanged. But the CIFAR arm exposed a second one that is now competitive with it, and is
cheaper to close: **the empirical claims are confounded across three axes at once.** CIFAR-10
differs from the grayscale corpora in image statistics, in output-channel count (c = 3 vs 1) and in
fit budget (1000 vs 300 steps). The third is dead (travel is indistinguishable — G5 notebook). The
second is the mechanism P-C1-B named for W10's rise and it resolved correctly, but it is not
*isolated* from the first. Two experiments separate them, and both are affordable: a no-new-fitting
channel ablation of the W10 encoding (`scripts/25_w10_channel_ablation.py`, wired), and a grayscale
CIFAR corpus at c = 1 (one corpus generation). Until one of them lands, "the recovery fractions
depend on signal complexity" is stated as an observation across three corpora, not as an isolated
effect of complexity.
