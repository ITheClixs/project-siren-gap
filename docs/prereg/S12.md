# S12 Pre-registration — the converged-fit ladder — FROZEN

**Study:** S12. Answers the one objection two external reviews have both called acceptance-critical:
every corpus in this program is fitted by constant-step Adam and none reaches stationarity, so no
result here is known to describe *converged* INRs. Ledger rows `H-S12-*`, `P-S12-*`. Written
before the converged fitter is implemented and before any corpus is generated.

---

## 1. What S8 established, and what it could not

S8 varied the step budget over $\{300, 1000, 3000, 10000\}$ and found $f(\calign)$ moving only
$-0.043$ across a $33\times$ range. But P-S8-C failed: the median relative endpoint gradient norm
ratio between $300$ and $10000$ steps was $1.17$ on `P-random` and $0.91$ on `P-shared-det`, so the
sweep never approached stationarity. `S8-addendum-02` traced that to the optimizer — constant-step
Adam does not shrink its step as the gradient shrinks, so past the end of descent the iterate
diffuses in a band set by the learning rate. **More steps cannot fix this. A different fitter can.**

## 2. Design

Two changes to the fitter, both standard:

1. **Cosine-decayed learning rate** from $10^{-3}$ to $10^{-6}$ over the budget, so the step size
   goes to zero with the schedule rather than sitting at a noise floor.
2. **A per-INR stationarity stopping rule**: an INR stops updating once its own relative endpoint
   gradient norm falls below $\tau = 10^{-4}$, which is more than an order of magnitude below
   anything S8 reached.

Everything else is frozen at the existing protocol: $L=2$, width $32$, $\omega_0$ absorbed, same
splits by image, same seed policy, same quality gates reported. Corpus size is the S8-reduced
$10$k/$2$k/$2$k so the study is affordable at three independent replications.

**Three independent corpus generations** per protocol, with disjoint corpus seeds. This is the
second thing the reviews asked for: it gives a between-corpus variance component, so the intervals
this program reports stop resolving only decoder-seed noise.

**Arms decoded:** W1, W3, W5 (the reframing), W12 (the group-aware reader). W1 and W3 fix the
anchors; W5 and W12 are the two claims most exposed to the convergence objection.

## 3. Validity conditions, checked before any decoding

The corpus is admitted only if **all** hold. If any fails, the study is reported as failed to
produce converged corpora and no ladder number from it is quoted.

- median relative endpoint gradient norm $< 10^{-4}$ on **both** protocols (S8 reached $6\times10^{-3}$);
- median render PSNR within $2$ dB **between** protocols, so a fit-quality difference cannot be
  mistaken for a symmetry effect (S8's 10000-step arm: $58.3$ vs $58.4$ dB, which passes);
- the existing render-classifier gate passes on both.

## 5. What the outcomes license (pre-committed)

- **If $f(\calign)$ converged falls below $0.15$**, the recoverable fraction is a property of the
  early-stopped regime. Every ladder claim in the paper is then rescoped to that regime **in the
  abstract**, and this becomes the paper's headline limitation rather than a robustness check.
- **If $f(\calign)$ converged is inside H-S12-4's interval**, the ladder numbers are reported as
  surviving convergence at the reduced corpus size, and the manuscript's blanket scoping to
  "constant-step Adam, which does not reach stationarity" is narrowed to the corpora it actually
  describes.
- **If $s(\mathrm{W12})$ converged drops below $0.65$**, the architectural claim is scoped to
  non-stationary fits, and the abstract says so.
- **If the validity conditions of §3 fail**, nothing is decoded and the failure is reported. We do
  not tune the schedule against a decoded number.
- **If H-S12-7 exceeds the decoder-seed spread by $3\times$ or more**, every interval in the paper
  is re-stated with a corpus-level component, and the calibration appendix records that the
  program's earlier intervals were too narrow for that reason.

## 6. Analysis

$f$ against the converged corpus's own W1 and W3 anchors, never against the existing ones.
Between-corpus variance from the three replications, reported alongside the decoder-seed bootstrap
so the two levels are visibly separate. No result from this study is compared to a non-converged
number without saying that the corpora differ.

**Budget.** Fitting is expected to cost more per INR than the 10000-step arm because the stopping
rule is a floor and not a cap; estimate $8$–$14$ h total for three replications of both protocols
at the reduced size, plus roughly $2$ h of decoding.
