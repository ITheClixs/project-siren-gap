# S6 Pre-registration — the orbit-only intervention — FROZEN

**Study:** S6. The causal counterpart to the S1 ladder, added in response to external review.
**Frozen:** 2026-08-02. Ledger rows `H-S6-*`, `P-S6-*`.

---

## 1. Why

S1 measures $f$, the share of the shared-versus-random *accuracy* gap that a feature map recovers.
An earlier version of this program read $f$ as a certified lower bound on the symmetry-caused share
of the gap. That reading is invalid: an orbit-valued map preserves the function but can still route
an orbit-invariant property into a coordinate the reader finds accessible (apply $\tau_k$ at the
first neuron with $k = M\,y(\theta)$ for an orbit-invariant binary $y$ — the function is untouched
and a linear probe now predicts $y$). The ladder's contrast also intervenes on the *initialization*,
i.e. on the fit map, not on the group.

S6 measures the causal quantity by intervening on the group directly.

## 2. Design

Take a corpus with **no** initialization nuisance (`P-shared-det`). Hold each fitted network and its
realised function fixed. Draw an independent group element per INR and apply it:

$$\theta_i \longmapsto g_i\theta_i,\qquad g_i \sim \mu_B \ \text{i.i.d.}$$

The same networks and the same functions appear on both sides — the residual functional gap is
verified at machine precision on every cell — so any degradation is caused by the group action and
nothing else. Define

$$\Delta_{\mathrm{sym}}(A,\mu_B) \;=\; \mathrm{acc}_A(\Theta) - \mathrm{acc}_A(\mu_B \cdot \Theta),$$

and score each treatment by the share of $\Delta_{\mathrm{sym}}$ it recovers.

**There is no uniform probability measure on $D_\infty$**, so $\mu$ is a family, not a distribution:
windings $j\sim\mathrm{Unif}\{-B..B\}$, signs $d\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$, permutations uniform on
$S_n$ (or identity, in the second arm). Every quantity is reported as a function of $B$.

**Arms.** (i) permuted, $B\in\{0,1,3,10\}$; (ii) identity permutation, same $B$ — isolates the
$D_\infty$ part from the permutation part; (iii) equivariant readers W11a/W11b at one representative
$B$; (iv) the same intervention applied to `P-random`, which asks whether an
independently-initialised corpus is *already* as group-scattered as random group elements make it.

**Treatments:** raw, $\csort$, $\calign$, the exact invariant encoding, and the two equivariant
readers — the full list the review asked for.

## 3. Declared exposure

**Arm (i) was run before this file was written and its numbers were seen** at $B=0,1,3$ and
partially at $B=10$: $\Delta_{\mathrm{sym}} \approx 79$ points, with $\csort$ recovering $\approx58\%$,
$\calign\approx87\%$, the invariant encoding $\approx73\%$. Arm (i) is therefore reported as a
**measurement, not a scored forecast**, and carries no ledger rows. This is proportionate: the
estimand is defined by the intervention and its value does not depend on having been predicted.

Arms (ii), (iii) and (iv) had not been run when this was frozen, and carry the registered rows below.

**Probability calls.**
- **P-S6-A = 0.80.** The permutation part dominates: $\Delta_{\mathrm{sym}}$(identity, $B{=}3$) is

- **P-S6-B = 0.85.** Adding scatter to `P-random` costs less than 2 points, i.e. an
  independently-initialised corpus is already close to group-saturated.

## 5. What the outcomes license (pre-committed)

- **$\calign$ recovers markedly more of $\Delta_{\mathrm{sym}}$ than of the observed gap.** Then the
  observed gap contains structure the group does not, and the difference between the two recovery
  fractions is the evidence for it — the inference the ladder alone could not support. It must be
  stated with the caveat that the scattered corpus is a *favourable* case for $\calign$ by
  construction, since every scattered network is exactly a group image of one fitted from the
  template's own initialization.
- **The two recovery fractions agree.** Then the observed gap behaves like pure group scatter for
  this algorithm, and the "not wholly symmetry" language stays withdrawn.
- **P-S6-A holds.** The novelty framing changes: $D_\infty\wr S_n$ is the correct group and the
  phase component is genuinely outside monomial actions, but its empirical contribution at this
  scale is small, and the abstract must say so rather than implying the affine part is what makes
  the problem hard.

## 6. Analysis and compute

Same frozen decoder, splits and seed policy as S1 ($n=5$). Equivariant readers at the
capacity-matched widths of `S1-w11.md` (424 / 288). Functional gap asserted at machine precision on
every cell; a cell whose gap exceeds $10^{-4}$ is void. Budget: arms (ii) and (iv) $\approx40$ min
each; arm (iii) $\approx85$ min.
