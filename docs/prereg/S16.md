# S16 Pre-registration — function-side nuisance control with seeds — FROZEN

**Study:** S16. Replaces the single-seed control behind the paper's "5.4-point fit-quality movement
seen from the function side".
**Frozen:** 2026-09-13, before any S16 cell is computed. Ledger rows `H-S16-*`, `P-S16-*`.

## 1. Why

The paper compares the 7.8-point shared-versus-random loss of its strongest invariant reader with
the movement of function access between the same two corpora. That movement (5.43 points) comes
from one seed per protocol at $K = 16$ probes (`scripts/35_s5_pareto.py`, `--nuisance-control`),
where the seed standard deviation is about 1.8 points, and $K = 16$ is not the operating point the
paper headlines ($K = 64$).

## 2. Design

MNIST, the frozen learned-probe reader of S5 (`train_probe_reader`), $K \in \{16, 64, 256\}$,
protocols `P-random` and `P-shared-det`, 5 seeds each (seed $s$ on both protocols forms a pair).
Estimand per $K$: $D_K = \mathrm{acc}(\texttt{P-shared-det}) - \mathrm{acc}(\texttt{P-random})$,
mean over paired seeds, with a bootstrap 95% interval over the five paired differences.

**Exposure (declared).** $K = 16$, seed 0 on both protocols gave $D_{16} = 5.43$; the S5 sweep on
`P-random` is known for all three $K$ (5 seeds). No `P-shared-det` value other than $K = 16$,
seed 0 has been computed.
