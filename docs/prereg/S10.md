## 1. The question

W12 changes **two** things at once against W11a, the permutation-equivariant reader on raw
weights:

1. **coordinates** — the bias is lifted to its phasor, $b \mapsto (\cos b, \sin b, \cos 2b,
   \sin 2b)$, which reduces the infinite winding to a parity;
2. **architecture** — those coordinates are read by a graded two-round message-passing skeleton
   with the layer-2 weight matrix as an edge coupling.

W11a $\to$ W12 is $0.265 \to 0.917$, and the matched ungraded control W12u ($0.858$) already
showed that the *layer-level grading* is worth only $+0.059$ of that. What no arm has separated is
the **coordinates** from the **skeleton**: W12u changed the grading but kept the phasor lift.

**W12b** is the converse control. Identical skeleton, identical block structure, identical edge
coupling, capacity re-solved by the same rule; the only change is that the bias enters **raw**
instead of lifted. Every trigonometric channel of $b$ is dropped and $b$ itself is placed once, in
the sign-covariant block $(1,0)$ — where the naive reading that ignores the winding would put it.

## 2. What it necessarily is

W12b is **not** invariant, and that is the design, not a defect: a winding $b \mapsto b + \pi j$ is
exactly what the phasor lift quotients and what a raw bias cannot. T18 asserts it moves under a
pure winding while W12 does not, so the arm cannot repeat the failure mode that would have made
W12u vacuous.

Note a structural consequence, stated in advance so it is not offered as an excuse afterwards:
under the grading, only the neutral block reaches the head, so information carried by a
**non-character** feature in $(1,0)$ reaches the head only through even combinations formed by the
bilinear rounds. W12b is therefore handicapped relative to an unconstrained reader on the same
inputs. That is intrinsic to the control being *the same graded skeleton*, which is what the
limitation asked for; a fourth arm without the grading would measure something else.

  $0.265 \to 0.858$ step.

## 4. What the outcomes license (pre-committed)

- **If $f(\mathrm{W12b}) \ge 0.75$:** the architecture, not the coordinates, carries most of the
  step. CLAIMS row 51's reading — that the win belongs to the symmetry-adapted coordinates — is
  **withdrawn rather than qualified**, and the paper's §15 sentence naming the coordinates as the
  mechanism is rewritten to name the reader architecture.
- **If $f(\mathrm{W12b}) \le 0.55$:** the coordinates carry it. Row 51 stands, the limitation
  closes, and the paper reports the three-arm decomposition as measured.
- **If $0.55 < f(\mathrm{W12b}) < 0.75$:** both contribute materially and neither dominates. The
  paper reports the arm's number and states that the attribution is split with no clean
  assignment — it does **not** pick whichever side is closer.
- **In every branch**, the grading handicap of §2 is restated where the number is reported, and the
  arm is described as bounding the architecture's contribution *within the graded skeleton*, not in
  general.
