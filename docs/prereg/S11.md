# S11 Pre-registration — the 2×2 and W12 off MNIST — FROZEN

**Study:** S11. Answers two objections raised in external review of the manuscript: that the W12
attribution is not factorial (review §3.11) and that the strongest reader is evaluated on one
dataset only (§3.5). Ledger rows `H-S11-*`, `P-S11-*`. Written before any of the four runs below
is launched; the only prior execution is a one-epoch CPU smoke of the new arm, whose artifact was
deleted (§5).

---

## 1. The two designs

**(a) Complete the 2×2.** W12's ingredients are the phasor lift of the bias and the graded
skeleton that reads it. Three cells exist:

| | raw bias | phasor bias |
|---|---|---|
| ungraded skeleton | **W12ub — not yet run** | W12u, $f = 0.858$ |
| graded skeleton | W12b, $f = 0.602$ | W12, $f = 0.917$ |

Without the fourth cell the two "main effects" are each measured along one edge of the square and
their interaction is unmeasured. W12ub fills it: no grading, raw bias, same skeleton, capacity
re-solved by the same rule.

**(b) W12 on the other three corpora.** MNIST is where the phasor reader was designed, after
seeing the MNIST ladder. FashionMNIST, luminance CIFAR-10 and RGB CIFAR-10 are the corpora on
which this program has already shown the *ordering* of treatments reversing (paper
\S\ref{sec:crossover}: $\calign$ leads on grayscale, the invariant encoding leads on CIFAR-10), so
they are the fair test of whether the architectural claim generalizes.

## 2. Registered quantities

Same protocol as every other rung: `P-random`, $n=5$ decoder seeds, capacity by the same rule
against each dataset's frozen decoder, $f$ computed against that dataset's own W1 and W3 anchors.

| id | quantity | point | 80% interval | reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| H-S11-1 | $f(\mathrm{W12ub})$, MNIST | 0.55 | [0.30, 0.78] | it keeps the skeleton and loses both the lift and the grading; expected between W11a's 0.265 and W12b's 0.602, but the ungraded head sees all four blocks, which helped W12u |
| H-S11-2 | interaction $[f(\mathrm{W12}) - f(\mathrm{W12b})] - [f(\mathrm{W12u}) - f(\mathrm{W12ub})]$ | 0.00 | [$-0.25$, $+0.25$] | no reason to expect the phasor lift to pay differently with the grading on than off |
| H-S11-3 | $f(\mathrm{W12})$, FashionMNIST | 0.85 | [0.60, 0.95] | grayscale, same fit budget and architecture as MNIST, where it reached 0.917 |
| H-S11-4 | $f(\mathrm{W12})$, luminance CIFAR-10 | 0.60 | [0.30, 0.85] | natural images; the encoding family already does better here than the reframing family |
| H-S11-5 | $f(\mathrm{W12})$, RGB CIFAR-10 | 0.60 | [0.30, 0.85] | as above; $c=3$ enters the reader through $u$, which the grading already carries |

**Probability calls.**
- **P-S11-A = 0.80.** $f(\mathrm{W12})$ exceeds the best exact reframing on *every* dataset, i.e.
  beats $\calign$'s 0.628 / 0.664 / 0.324.
- **P-S11-B = 0.55.** $f(\mathrm{W12})$ on RGB CIFAR-10 exceeds the invariant encoding W10's 0.534.
- **P-S11-C = 0.30.** The interaction of H-S11-2 is outside $[-0.10, +0.10]$, i.e. the two
  ingredients are not close to additive.

## 3. What the outcomes license (pre-committed)

- **If $f(\mathrm{W12})$ beats $\calign$ on all four corpora**, the architectural claim is reported
  as holding across the datasets tested, and the manuscript's scoping to MNIST is lifted for that
  claim only.
- **If it fails on any corpus**, the abstract states the failure and the claim is scoped to the
  corpora where it holds. It is not repaired by tuning: the capacity rule, optimizer, schedule and
  seed policy are frozen exactly as for MNIST.
- **If the interaction of H-S11-2 falls outside $[-0.25, +0.25]$**, the additive language
  ("coordinates contribute $+0.315$, architecture $+0.337$") is withdrawn and replaced by the 2×2
  table with the interaction reported, because non-additivity makes single-number main effects
  misleading.
- **In every branch**, W12b's grading handicap (S10 §2) still applies to any statement about the
  graded row of the table.

## 4. Analysis

$f$ against each dataset's own anchors, bootstrap CIs over the five paired seeds. The invariance
audit is run per dataset for W12 and reported with the accuracy, as for MNIST. No dataset's result
is used to modify the architecture: the construction is frozen at the MNIST version.

## 5. Declared exposure

A one-epoch, one-seed CPU smoke of the `--ungraded --raw-bias` combination was run to check the
artifact write path and the capacity rule (which returns width 122, 1,885,284 parameters). It
reported a one-epoch accuracy, which is not an estimate of any registered quantity here, and its
artifact was deleted.

**Budget.** Roughly $1.7$ h for W12ub and $1.5$–$2$ h per dataset for the three W12 runs, serial.
