# S19 Pre-registration — the review-round controls — FROZEN

**Study:** S19. Nine arms answering an external review of the TMLR manuscript.
**Frozen:** 2026-09-15, before any S19 cell is computed. Ledger rows `H-S19-*`, `P-S19-*`.

## Why

The review accepted the measurements but found that several readings go beyond them. The residual
comparison across widths mixes corpus sizes. The "not lost information" reading of the residual
rests on a saturated probe count. The orbit intervention was never tested at partial scatter, and its
non-group control touches coordinates the relabelling does not. The reader claims rest on an in-house
permutation-equivariant baseline far weaker than published readers, and on no control against the
existing bias-folding remedy. The genericity diagnostic measures the wrong stratum. Each arm below
answers one of these.

## Arms

**A1 — W12 at width 32 on the 10k corpora.** `scripts/47_w12_phasor.py`, unchanged, on
`P-random-s8s300` and `P-shared-det-s8s300` (MNIST, 10k/2k/2k, 300 steps, the corpora of the S8
300-step arm and S17 draw 0), five seeds, the same capacity rule, anchors W1 = 88.50 and W3 = 10.86
(the S8 300-step cells). Quantities: $s_{10k}(\mathrm{W12})$ and the residual
$\mathrm{acc}(\texttt{P-shared-det-s8s300}) - \mathrm{acc}(\texttt{P-random-s8s300})$.

**A2 — W12 on the shared-initialization corpora of FashionMNIST and CIFAR-10.** The same script on
`P-shared-det` of each, five seeds; the residual is the difference from the existing W12 cell on
`P-random` of the same dataset.

**A3 — Partial scatter and a second non-group control (MNIST, `P-shared-det`).** The frozen decoder
recipe of S15, five seeds per cell. Sign dose: every hidden neuron independently receives a sign
$d\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$ with probability $p$, and $d=0$ otherwise. Relabelling dose: in each
hidden layer a uniformly chosen subset of $\mathrm{round}(pn)$ neurons is permuted uniformly among
itself, re-wiring consistently. $p\in\{0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 1\}$ for each, so $p=1$ reproduces the S15
single-generator cells. Control: per INR, the rows of $W^2$ and the entries of $b^2$ are permuted
without re-wiring the columns of $W^3$, which changes the function and touches exactly the
coordinates a layer-two relabelling touches.

**A4 — Function access with probes fixed at pixel centres.** S16's design with the probes frozen at
$K$ fit-grid points (the deterministic subsample `probe_coords` uses), $K\in\{16,64\}$, both MNIST
protocols, five paired seeds. Quantity: the shared-minus-random difference at each $K$.

**A5 — Off-grid functional discrepancy (analysis, no training).** For test images present in
`P-random-K`, the RMS difference between two independent fits of the same image, on the $28\times28$
fit grid and on the interleaved off-grid points of the $55\times55$ grid, each divided by the RMS of
the fitted image. Quantity: the off-grid over on-grid ratio of that discrepancy (median over images).

**A6 — Fold then grade.** Every hidden bias is folded into $[-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ by the element $g_{0,j}$
with the compensating sign on the outgoing weights (the canonical-form version of ScaleGMN's
Algorithm 1), then W12's graded skeleton reads the folded bias raw, in the $(1,0)$ block (the W12b
feature map). MNIST `P-random`, five seeds, the same capacity rule. Invariant off the fold's
boundary, a null set.

**A7 — Genericity, measured on the right stratum (analysis).** $\delta(\theta)=\min_{i\ne j}\min
(\|w_i-w_j\|,\|w_i+w_j\|)/\mathrm{median}_i\|w_i\|$ on the first layer, for fitted `P-random` INRs and
for fresh draws from the same initialization distribution, MNIST.

**A8 — Benchmark bias range (analysis).** The fraction of $\omega_0$-absorbed hidden biases outside
$[-\pi/2,\pi/2)$ in each published benchmark corpus, per layer.

**A9 — Published readers on the in-house corpora.** NG-GNN (neural-graphs) and ScaleGMN from their
authors' public code, with their INR-classification hyperparameters, on MNIST `P-random` and
`P-shared-det`, the in-house splits, three seeds each. Weights enter in the canonical form the
corpora are stored in; ScaleGMN applies its own bias fold.

## Exposure

Every existing cell is known, including S16's learned-probe differences (6.44 at $K=16$, $-0.25$ at
$K=64$), the S8 300-step anchors, W12 on `P-random` for all four corpora, and the published
leaderboard. No S19 cell has been computed, and neither published reader has been run on any
in-house corpus.

## What the paper will say

Each quantity is reported whatever it is. Four readings are fixed now.

- If the 10k residual (A1) differs from the 10k width-128 residual by less than 2 points, the paper
  says the residual does not change with width at matched corpus size; otherwise it reports both and
  withdraws the width statement.
- If the grid-probe difference at $K=16$ (A4) exceeds 3 points, the claim that the residual is not
  lost information is withdrawn, and the paper says a function-level difference between the corpora
  is visible at low probe counts.
- If either published reader (A9) reaches $s \ge s(\mathrm{W12}) - 0.05$ on `P-random`, the reader
  contribution is narrowed to the phasor lift and the paper is framed as a measurement study.
- If fold-then-grade (A6) is within 0.03 of W12, the paper says continuity buys nothing measurable on
  these corpora.
