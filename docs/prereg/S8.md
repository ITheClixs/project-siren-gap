# S8 Pre-registration — the convergence sweep — FROZEN

**Study:** S8. Priority 6 of the external review. Ledger rows `H-S8-*`, `P-S8-*`.
**Frozen:** 2026-08-03, after corpus generation began and **before any cell was decoded**. The
corpora are an intervention on the fit map and carry no outcome; the registered quantities are all
decoded ones.

---

## 1. Why

Every ladder result in this program is measured on fits that end **unconverged**. The one-neuron
microcosm at the corpus budget ends with endpoint gradient norms of $0.5$–$0.7$; the production
corpora at 300 steps end with a median relative gradient norm of $5.3\times10^{-3}$. The review put
the objection precisely:

> If alignment recovery disappears after convergence, the correct conclusion is that symmetry
> dominates in the early-stopped local-fit regime, not in independently fitted INRs generally.

Render fidelity does not settle this. A network can interpolate the sampled grid while sitting far
from a stationary point, and at width 32 depth 2 an MNIST INR has 1185 parameters against 784
targets, so it *can* interpolate. A pilot confirms the two measures come apart: 300 steps gives
$37.3$\,dB and 1000 steps gives $63.6$\,dB, while both remain far from stationarity.

## 2. Design

Same images, same architecture ($w=32$, $L=2$, $\omega_0$ absorbed), same optimizer (Adam
$10^{-3}$, full batch, deterministic), same seed policy. Only the **step budget** changes:

$$\text{steps} \in \{300,\ 1000,\ 3000,\ 10000\}, \qquad
\text{protocol} \in \{\texttt{P-shared-det},\ \texttt{P-random}\}.$$

Corpus size is reduced to 10k train / 2k val / 2k test so the 10000-step arm is affordable. **The
300-step arm at this reduced size is the internal control**; comparisons are within the sweep, never
against the full-corpus ladder, whose W1 is measured on 55k training rows.

Decoded at every budget with the frozen apparatus: **W1** (shared-det raw), **W3** (random raw),
**W4** ($\csort$), **W5** ($\calign$), **W10** (exact invariants). Reported alongside, from the
corpus metadata and with no new fitting: median relative endpoint gradient norm, median render
PSNR, and median relative parameter travel $\|\theta_T - \theta_0\|/\|\theta_0\|$.

The endpoint gradient norm is $\|\nabla_\theta \mathcal L_i(\theta)\| / \|\theta\|$ for each INR's
*own* full-batch loss, added to the fitter for this study and asserted by T4 to fall with the budget.

## 4. What the outcomes license (pre-committed)

- **Falsifier.** If $f(\mathrm{W5})$ at 10000 steps is below $0.15$, the recoverable fraction is a
  property of the early-stopped regime. The paper must then say so in the abstract, rescope every
  ladder claim to that regime, and report the sweep as the headline limitation rather than as a
  robustness check.
- **If $f(\mathrm{W5})$ declines but stays above 0.30**, the decline is reported as a real budget
  dependence with its magnitude, and the ladder's numbers are labelled as measured at 300 steps.
- **If $f(\mathrm{W5})$ is flat in the budget**, that is stated plainly and the mechanism section's
  claim --- that the fit map's output is dominated by its initialization argument --- is reported as
  surviving a direct test rather than as an inference from displacement.
- **If P-S8-C fails**, the sweep did not reach stationarity and cannot answer the review's question;
  we say that instead of reading the accuracy numbers as though it had.

## 5. Analysis and compute

Same frozen decoder, splits and seed policy as S1 ($n=5$). Quality gates are *not* applied as
admission criteria here, because over-fitting at high budgets is the intervention rather than a
defect; render PSNR is reported per budget instead. Corpus generation measured at $\approx6\times10^{-5}$\,s
per fit-step: 4 / 14 / 41 / 138 minutes per protocol, $\approx6.7$ hours in total. Decoding is
$\approx15$ minutes.

**Optional arm, not registered.** A plain-gradient-descent fitter at one budget would separate
"Adam's trajectory" from "the budget". Our Adam is already deterministic and full-batch, which is
what the review asked for; if the GD arm is run it is exploratory and marked as such.
