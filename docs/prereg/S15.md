# S15 Pre-registration — factorial orbit intervention — FROZEN

**Study:** S15. Identifies how the orbit-only intervention's damage splits across the four
generators, which the nested arms of S6 cannot do.
**Frozen:** 2026-09-13, before any S15 cell is computed. Ledger rows `H-S15-*`, `P-S15-*`.

## 1. Why

S6 reported the damage of randomizing the group on `P-shared-det` (MNIST) as nested increments:
sign flips alone about 63 points, adding windings about 1, adding the permutation about 15. Nested
increments depend on the order in which the arms are added, and a symmetry-blind decoder saturates
near its floor, so the increments are not an attribution. S6 also used one group draw.

## 2. Design

Corpus `P-shared-det`, MNIST, the frozen raw-weight decoder (`train_matched_mlp`), 5 decoder seeds,
exactly as S6. Per INR, draw an independent element of the group with four independently switchable
factors, applied to every hidden layer:

- $\sigma$: $d \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$, else $d = 0$;
- $\rho$: winding parity $r \sim \mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$, else $r = 0$;
- $\tau$: even winding $2m$ with $m \sim \mathrm{Unif}\{-5..5\}$, else $m = 0$;
- $\pi$: permutation uniform on $S_n$, else identity;

with $j = 2m + r$. All $2^4 - 1 = 15$ non-empty cells are decoded, plus the untouched baseline.
Every cell's functional gap is checked on the test split; a cell with gap above $10^{-4}$ is void.

Two further arms:
- **Draw replicates:** the all-factors cell under two additional group seeds (5678, 9012) besides
  the main seed 1234.
- **Non-group scramble:** permute the first-layer neurons (rows of $W^1$, $b^1$) per INR *without*
  permuting the columns of $W^2$. This changes the function, and measures how far any
  coordinate scramble of that size takes the decoder.

**Estimands.** $\Delta(S) = \mathrm{acc}(\text{baseline}) - \mathrm{acc}(S)$ for each factor set
$S$; Shapley values $\phi_\sigma, \phi_\rho, \phi_\tau, \phi_\pi$ of the set function $\Delta$, which
sum to $\Delta(\{\sigma,\rho,\tau,\pi\})$ by construction.

**Exposure (declared).** Two cells are close to cells already measured in S6: $\{\sigma\}$ (S6
identity-permutation arm at $B=0$: 62.90) and the full cell (S6 permuted arm, $j \sim
\mathrm{Unif}\{-10..10\}$: 79.09). Their predictions below are anchored on those values and are not
scored as tests. Nothing else in this design has been computed.

## 4. What the paper will say, fixed in advance

- The body replaces the nested increments with the four Shapley values and their intervals, and
  states that they are one of several possible attributions.
- If P-S15-B is false, the sentence "sign flips carry about 63 points" is removed wherever it
  appears and replaced by the Shapley ordering.
- If $\phi_\tau \ge 5$, the sentence that the affine phase component "costs about one point" is
  withdrawn and replaced by $\phi_\tau$ (and $\phi_\rho$).
- If the non-group scramble's $\Delta$ is within 5 points of the full cell's, the body states that
  the 79-point figure is at the decoder's floor and is not specific to the group.
