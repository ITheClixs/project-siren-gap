# Advisor Committee Review — Gate G3 (INR-Bench sine corpora)

Date: 2026-07-28. Scope: the MNIST / FashionMNIST / CIFAR-10 sine corpora, their task-referenced
quality gates, the leakage and strata audits, replication anchors A1/A2, the CIFAR pilot sweep and
the R-CIFAR path decision, and the compute ledger.

## Empiricist

1. **The gate is task-referenced, but the reference moved between datasets.** MNIST/FMNIST used a
   2-epoch CNN (99 / 86 % real-pixel); CIFAR-10 needed 10 epochs to reach 67 % and the gate is only
   as discriminating as its reference. This was declared in the pilot memo *before* the sweep and
   the cache key carries the epoch count, so no earlier number moved — but the thesis must say
   plainly that a 1.0-pt margin against a 67 %-accurate reference is a weaker certificate than the
   same margin against a 99 %-accurate one. [Response: accepted; Ch4 gets a sentence and the gate
   table reports the reference accuracy in every row.]
2. **CIFAR renders lose to pixels; MNIST renders beat them.** QG-8 registered the sign flip and it
   happened (+0.25 pts on CIFAR at the frozen config vs −0.05 to −0.07 on MNIST/FMNIST). The
   explanation on offer — INR low-pass smoothing removes pixel noise the small CNN cannot handle,
   which helps on digits and costs on natural texture — is post-hoc as a *mechanism* even though the
   direction was pre-registered. [Response: accepted, and it is labelled as such: the sign was
   registered, the mechanism is a conjecture and is written as one.]
3. **Two configs passed the CIFAR gate and the cheaper one has 28 dB less PSNR** (w32 L2 s1000 at
   40.1 dB, gap +0.25; w64 L3 s500 at 68.1 dB, gap +0.05). Freezing the cheaper one is what the
   pre-committed rule says, and it keeps the architecture identical to MNIST/FMNIST, which is worth
   more for cross-dataset comparability than the extra fidelity. But a reader will ask whether S1's
   CIFAR arm inherits a weaker corpus. [Response: accepted; the 68 dB config is retained as a
   registered sensitivity arm to be run if any CIFAR rung lands within 2 pts of a decision
   boundary.]
4. **The leakage sign flips across datasets and now within CIFAR** (corr(PSNR, label): MNIST-det
   +0.203, FMNIST-det −0.067, CIFAR-det −0.138). That is evidence it is fit-difficulty structure
   rather than one artifact, but the S1 PSNR-matched control is pre-committed only for the headline
   rungs. [Response: accepted as scoped; the trigger in the S1 registration is a *test*, not a
   judgement call, so it fires or does not fire on its own.]
5. **The compute ledger was wrong by an order of magnitude** until this session: 123.4 h of
   wall-clock was recorded where 11.1 h of compute happened, because detached shards absorb machine
   sleep. Fixed by measuring median s/fit × fits. [Response: accepted; both clocks are now in the
   ledger and the budget uses the active one.]

## Theorist

1. **A1's 80-point gap is partly an artifact of the reader, and the ladder now proves it.** A plain
   MLP has no permutation structure, so W3 is floor-level by construction; the honest quantity is
   what survives an exact change of frame, and that is W5 = 64.4. The corpora are therefore fine,
   but every statement of the form "the weight space destroys 80 points of information" must be
   written as "for a matched permutation-blind reader". [Response: accepted; it is stated that way
   in the S1 registration §8.3 and in the notebook.]
2. **The strata audit shows production fits pass Θ_gen only marginally** (minimal parallel angles
   ~3e−4 rad at layer 1). Identifiability is not in danger, conditioning is. [Response: accepted;
   this is defense row 3 and F7's job.]

## Statistician

1. **QG-3 missed by 50 points and QG-5 missed on the high side; the calibration story is now a
   pattern, not an incident.** Two of the three big misses ran the same direction: hedging a
   registered mechanism toward literature priors from a different reader class. [Response:
   accepted; recorded in the notebook and in defense row 14, and the running coverage is reported
   rather than the successes alone.]
2. **The pilot's throughput measurement is the input to a binding decision rule and it was taken on
   a machine that slept mid-sweep.** A single clean shard drove the estimate. [Response: accepted —
   a dedicated clean-throughput measurement was taken at the frozen config before applying R-CIFAR,
   and the decision has ~2× headroom against its threshold either way.]
3. **The registered corpus arithmetic in R-CIFAR undercounted** (3 × 50 000 assumed 50k per
   protocol; the actual per-protocol total is 60k = 45k train + 5k val + 10k test, so the full path
   is 540k fits, not 510k). [Response: accepted and logged; the decision is unchanged under either
   count, and the corrected figure is what the ledger carries.]

## Verdict

**G3 PASSES.** All four protocols on MNIST and FashionMNIST are generated and gate-green; anchors
A1 and A2 passed against criteria fixed before the data existed; CIFAR-10's config is frozen by a
pre-committed rule against a strengthened gate, and its corpus generation is a mechanical execution
of that frozen decision. Carry-forward items: (i) the retained 68 dB CIFAR sensitivity arm,
(ii) reporting the gate's reference accuracy in every gate row, (iii) the CIFAR corpus itself must
land and gate-green before any CIFAR rung of S1 is reported.
