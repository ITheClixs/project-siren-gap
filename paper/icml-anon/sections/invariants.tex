\section{Exact invariants at depth two}\label{sec:invariants}

Requiring monomial invariance $g(b)P_\alpha(w)Q_\beta(u)$ under \eqref{eq:normalform} forces $g$ to
be $2\pi$-periodic with $g(b+\pi) = (-1)^\beta g(b)$ and $g(-b)=(-1)^{\alpha+\beta}g(b)$, whose
minimal-frequency solutions give a separating per-neuron feature map on $\{w\neq0,u\neq0\}$:
\begin{equation}
\Phi(w,b,u) = \big(w\otimes w,\ \cos 2b,\ (\sin 2b)\,w,\ (\sin b)\,u,\ (\cos b)(w\otimes u)\big).
\label{eq:phi}
\end{equation}
The features one would guess, $\cos(2b)(w\otimes u)$ and $\sin(2b)(w\otimes u)$, are \emph{not}
invariant: the first has the wrong bias frequency for $\rho$, the second the wrong parity for
$\sigma$, and both are refuted numerically in our test suite.

Equation~\eqref{eq:phi} is scoped to $L=1$. At depth two a hidden neuron's outgoing vector is a
column of a matrix the next layer's group also acts on, so per-neuron constructions fail. Coupling
the layers repairs it.

\begin{proposition}[Exact $L=2$ invariant encoding]\label{prop:deep}
Let $G_2 = W_2^\top W_2$. Then $G_2$ is invariant under the entire layer-2 group and picks up
$\varepsilon_i \varepsilon_l$ under layer~1, with $\varepsilon_i = (-1)^{d_i + j_i}$. Since
$\sin b_i$ carries $\varepsilon_i$ and $\cos b_i w_i$ carries $\varepsilon_i$ after contraction, the
matrices $A = (\sin b_i \sin b_l)G_2$, $B = (\cos b_i w_i \cdot \cos b_l w_l)G_2$ and
$C = (\sin 2b_i w_i \cdot \sin 2b_l w_l)$ are sign-cancelling and transform as
$M \mapsto P M P^\top$ under the layer-1 permutation, so their sorted eigenvalue spectra are
invariant under the full product group with no genericity condition.
\end{proposition} The genericity the encoder does need is in the order statistics, since per-neuron even scalars are
sorted by the shared key $\|w_i\|^2$. Appendix~\ref{app:w10} gives the encoding coordinate by
coordinate and states what is not claimed: the family is invariant but not shown to separate orbits.
Random group elements with windings $|j|\le3$ and non-trivial permutations move it by
$3\times10^{-7}$ relative, which is fp32 round-off.
